01

The condition being tested

This audit concerns one bounded occurrence: a church institution establishes or maintains a house of worship after directly affected people have refused the specific imposition, and the institution has not resolved the harm through genuine consent, departure, or repair.

Opposition alone does not establish that condition. A lawful approval does not disprove it. The occurrence must be reconstructed from the complete site record.

FIELD:CONDITION
02

The speaking grain

This page does not speak as God and does not claim divine intent. It speaks from the Church’s retained religious grain: its published scriptures, its published teaching on repentance, and its observable institutional acts.

“All things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them, for this is the law and the prophets.”

3 Nephi 14:12 · official Church scripture

The same corpus requires confession and forsaking of sin. The Church’s own teaching adds restitution: repair the hurt or damage caused by the act.

The producing path

No conclusion is detached from the act that produced it.

  1. ROOTPublished command

    Do to others what you would have them do to you.

  2. ACTSite decision

    Acquire, design, seek approval, construct, and maintain the church occurrence.

  3. BOUNDARYAffected people

    Identify who bears the direct land-use effects and what they actually accepted or refused.

  4. COMPAREReciprocity

    Apply the institution’s requested religious and property protections in both directions.

  5. REPAIRRepentance test

    Confess, forsake, and repair the established wrong, if the wrong is established.

  6. RESULTClassify the occurrence

    Derived only when every necessary edge is evidenced.

04

The derived finding

If the Church knowingly imposes and continues a specific church occurrence on directly affected people while refusing them the reciprocal treatment its own command requires, and it neither forsakes nor repairs that act, then the continuing church occurrence is the continuing breach. Under the Church’s own religious classification, it is a sin carried on in the name of the grain the institution claims to obey.

This is a conditional derivation. It is not a finding about every temple, every disagreement, or every person involved.

FIELD:FINDING
05

Fairview, Texas: present public state

The official Church record says the original proposal was reduced through mediation, the Town Council approved the revised plan 5–2 in April 2025, construction began in February 2026, and disagreement continued afterward. Town records preserve public opposition and the municipality’s position.

Those facts establish conflict, negotiation, compromise, approval, and continuation. They do not, by themselves, establish the decisive condition above: an unresolved non-reciprocal imposition after the full effects, affected parties, authority, alternatives, and repair have been compared.

FIELD:FAIRVIEW
06

Repentance and repair

Confess. Name the specific act without substituting legality, institutional authority, or good intention for its effect.

Forsake. Stop the established breach. Continuation is not forsaking.

Restore. Repair the hurt or damage as far as possible. A private feeling cannot substitute for an observable repair where the act is public.

Verify. Return to the affected boundary and test whether the identified difference remains.

Church teaching: Repentance · Doctrine and Covenants 58:42–43
